Managing focus

When a tab opens, move focus to that section’s title (tabindex="-1"). Tab then starts inside the panel, so the user does not have to walk through Home, About, and Store before they can use the links.

About

The shop started in a rented kitchen above a bookstore. Two friends cooked for neighbors, then wrote the recipes down so people could make them at home. We still buy from the same two farms we used in that first year.
